Warden's Field Manual: Outpost 4
System Directive: Closed-Loop Bio-Industrial Agronomy & Ossuary Extraction

I. Homestead Agronomy

Your soil is dead limestone. Normal crops will not germinate without careful biochemical calibration:

  • Till Soil: Use Spade to break earth.
  • Irrigate: Refill water at the well. Keep beds moist (40-80%).
  • Compost: Deposit Zone carcasses in the Vat to brew nitrogen slurry.
  • Harvest: Mature glowing pods yield combat-grade ammunition.

II. Munitions & Apothecary

You cannot purchase ammunition; everything fired is grown in your soil:

  • Phosphor-Gourd: Crafts Phosphor Flare Slugs. Burns gloom shields & illuminates darkness.
  • Cinnabar Core: Crafts Heavy Kinetic Slugs. Delivers devastating pneumatic impact.
  • Iron-Sap Reed: Distills Suture Inhalers to restore health & sanity.

III. Exclusion Zone Extraction

Deploy through the Decontamination Airlock at the north gate into the pitch-black ruins:

  • Raymarched FoV: Horrors conceal themselves in absolute darkness outside your flashlight beam.
  • Gloom Shields: Stalkers absorb kinetic fire. Hit them with a Phosphor Flare or sustained light beam to shatter their shroud!
  • Extraction Winch: Spool the tether at the far side. Defend against attracted hordes until 100% ready.

IV. Controls Reference

Unified input scheme across both phases:

  • WASD: Locomotion | Shift: Sprint
  • Mouse Aim: Facing / Flashlight Vector
  • LMB / Space: Till / Water / Harvest / Fire Weapon
  • 1 - 6 or Q / E: Switch Tool or Weapon
  • E: Interact with Stations, Nodes, or Winch
  • F: Flashlight Toggle | R: Inhale Neuro-Suture
